## What changes

The S29 site view (both sides) gets a ground layer: roads with kerbs and a dashed centre line, walkways,
and car parks with stall lines and parked cars. Buildings, markers and picking are unchanged.

## Sources

- Roads traced from `Cpac map.png` (same scale and origin as the site blocks, D-126): highway 306 and the
  internal roads around อาคาร 1–5 on side 1; the road along Warehouse 1 and the road past Riverside 1 to
  the car park on side 2.
- Car parks: "ที่จอดรถ CPAC" (the rounded lot on the map, side 2); the others and the walkways are placed
  by Claude on open ground of the map (decorative, no business data).
