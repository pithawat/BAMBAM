### 1. Phones / tablets (below `lg`, 1024px): mobile-app shell + S03 pickers

- **Bottom tab bar** on every (app) route, settings included: `หน้าแรก` + the services the user may use (`usesService`, same links and order as the desktop navbar). Icon in a pill over the label; active = soft-blue pill + primary label. Fixed to the bottom, respects the iPhone safe area, and slides away while a text field has focus (on-screen keyboard), as native apps do.
- The top bar keeps the logo, the settings gear and the account avatar; its second row of services is removed.
- Route-local bottom bars sit on the tab bar (`--app-bottom`): S03 footer (ย้อนกลับ / ถัดไป), settings S30 label sheet action, settings roles save bar.
- **S03 step 1**: อาคาร, ชั้น and the room list ("เลือกจากรายชื่อ" / floors without a plan) are searchable dropdowns. On phones a dropdown opens as a bottom sheet with a search field (not auto-focused, so the keyboard does not cover the list). **Step 2**: หมวดงาน is a searchable dropdown with the category icons. Symptoms stay a checklist (multi-select). The floor plan picker (CR-038) is unchanged.
- S03 breadcrumb is hidden on phones (the tab bar and the footer's ยกเลิก cover it).
- **สแกน QR in the tab bar (owner, 2026-11-10 follow-up):** a raised round primary button in the middle of the tab bar, label `สแกน QR` → S16 `/repair/scan`; shown with the same rule as before (phone with camera + coarse pointer, `repair.ticket.report`). **Every other scan button is removed** (S02 Home, S06 `พบปัญหาในที่ทำงาน?` card). Owner: "สแกน QR ให้ทำเป็นปุ่มบน menu แทน" / "เอาปุ่มสแกน QR ที่ไม่ได้อยู่บน Menu ออกด้วย".

### 2. Desktop S03

- อาคาร is the same searchable dropdown (a menu under the field with a search box) instead of the building cards. Floor chips, room cards and category tiles stay.

### 3. S02 employee Home (every role without `repair.overview.view`)

- Layout and type from the owner reference `App (1).tsx` + `index (1).css` (stored at the repo root by the owner): **IBM Plex Sans Thai** on the Home, centred greeting `✦ สวัสดี คุณ{ชื่อ} | วันนี้ให้เราช่วยอะไรดี?`, H1 `เรื่องในที่ทำงาน` / `จัดการง่าย ในที่เดียว` (primary). **The sub-line is removed.**
- Desktop: three cards side by side, car tilted −4°, the repair card in the middle on the primary mascot panel, room tilted +4°; card height `clamp(240px, 34vh, 325px)`.
- The repair card carries a **`รายการของฉัน` strip** (report key only, from the reference): total of #35 · the first non-empty state (`รอคุณให้คะแนน` → `กำลังดำเนินการ` → `เสร็จสิ้น`) · `อีก {n} รายการ`; tap → S06. It replaces the CR-035 list of 3 rows under the cards.
- Phones / tablets: the reference keeps three cards in one row with 6–8px text, which breaks Thai words at any readable size. The repair card spans the row, car and room sit tilted under it; สแกน QR lives in the tab bar only (no button on the Home).
- Kept from the app (not copied from the reference): BAMBAM logo and names, permissions (no report key → `งานซ่อมของคุณ` / `ไปที่งานซ่อม`; car / room `เร็วๆ นี้` + disabled `ยังไม่เปิดให้บริการ`, as they are not open), the line drawings already in the app. Left out because they have no destination or are demo-only: the bell, `คู่มือการใช้งาน`, `ติดต่อผู้ดูแล`, the empty-view toggle and the reference's demo dialogs. Footer: `© {year} BAMBAM Workplace`.

## Why

Phones are the main device for reporting; the owner wants the app to feel like a mobile app and long lists to be searchable. The Home follows the owner's newer reference.
