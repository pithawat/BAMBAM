## What changes

Owner's words (2026-10-29, Thai → English):

1. **Redesign the settings pages** to the owner's design `App.tsx` + `index.css` (repo root, untracked).
   - When a settings group is opened, show **only that group's sub-menus**, not every group (the design's
     `category-tabs` row with all groups goes away; the `sub-navigation` of the open group stays).
   - In the navbar "ตั้งค่าระบบ" dropdown, the sub-menu items have **no `>` chevron**.
2. **More permissions** so the role matrix lets the administrator restrict users across the whole platform
   ("ให้ admin ดูแล platform ให้ได้มากที่สุด"). Show the list to the owner before building.
3. A permission to **view the 3D building model**.
4. **Technicians do not see the other services and cannot report repairs.** Reporting becomes a permission
   (some users must not report). Think platform-wide: repair + car booking + room booking.
5. **Pagination** on ประวัติการเปลี่ยนแปลง (S26).

## Why

The owner wants one place to control who can do what in every service, and a settings UI that matches the
new design.

Owner 2026-10-29: Admin manages roles and permissions of other people too, but never their own or Superadmin's.
Guard rules (code, not matrix; apply to everyone except Superadmin; API returns 403, UI shows the row locked
with the reason):

- G1 — cannot edit, copy-over or delete **their own role** in the matrix, and cannot change **their own** role.
- G2 — cannot touch **Superadmin**: the `super_admin` role is not editable, nobody can be given or lose
  `super_admin` except by Superadmin (and `SUPER_ADMIN_EMPLOYEE_CODES`).
- G3 — **no escalation**: can only tick / grant permissions they hold themselves, and can only assign a role
  whose permissions are all within their own (stops "make a stronger role, give it to a friend").
- G4 — every change is in ประวัติการเปลี่ยนแปลง (already true, F30).
